## CedClient 4.7.0 for Minecraft 26.3

Requires Fabric Loader 0.19.5+, Fabric API and Fabric Language Kotlin.

> 4.7.0 is for **Minecraft 26.3 only**. 26.1.2 and 26.2 stay on 4.6.9. Keybinds are migrated to 26.3's key codes the first time you start it.

This is the biggest update so far: the whole mod was ported to 26.3, got a new look, a second menu layout, and dozens of new modules. Everything below is compared with 4.6.9.

### New: look and menu
- **New look**: one dark card style for the menus and every HUD, a shared colour palette, and one **Accent** (Mod Settings > Accent) for headline numbers, the dot on enabled modules, sliders and the `[CC]` chat prefix. White means no accent.
- **Accent picker**: opens the first time you open the menu (8 swatches with a live preview). Skip or Esc keeps the accent you have. **Pick Accent** in Mod Settings opens it again.
- **Font** (Mod Settings): Inter or the game's own Minecraft pixel font, for the menus and the HUD cards.
- **Menu Layout** (Mod Settings): **Classic** (one panel per category, now with a module count in each title, scroll arrows and a hint when panels run past the screen edge) or **Compact** (a category list, the module list and the module's settings as three panels). Compact has **Menu Size** and **Menu Opacity**.
- The **Click GUI** row (the old Mod Settings) is highlighted in the module list, and shows as "Click GUI".
- Modules in each panel are sorted by name width, widest first. CamelCase names show with spaces ("Chat Filter"). Settings text is bigger.
- **Fixed HUD Size** (Mod Settings): HUD cards keep the widest size they have had, so they do not jump around while numbers change.
- **HUD editor** redone: a **HUD** button next to the search bar opens it (same as `/cc hud`). A pill at the top picks which HUDs are shown. Click a HUD to select it: its toolbar has `-`, `+`, Reset and **BG** (that HUD's card on or off), and scrolling also resizes it. Right-click cycles through every HUD stacked on the same spot. The grid (G) snaps every 5 pixels (it was 10) and its big lines meet the centre lines. The layout is kept per GUI size and every HUD stays pinned to its nearest screen edge.
- **HUD text** is see-through with a soft halo by default. The tracker HUDs (Slayer, Run Profit, Daily Tasks, Critter Safari, Music) keep a card. Colours that are too dark to read (Hypixel dark red, dark purple) are lifted. Your HUD backgrounds are reset once for the new look; positions and other settings stay.
- **Mod icon**: new duck head logo.

### New: Dungeons
- **Terminal Solver**: shows only the slots to click in the F7 terminals, in a clean panel. Options: First Click Delay, Click Delay, Wait For Server Answer, drop key and left / right click keys, a toggle per terminal, Click Sound, Show Items, panel scale, roundness, colours (default background #2B2B2B, border #CB0101). **Rubix Left Click Only** (any button clicks a lit tile and the right click is sent when needed). Melody: Prevent Wrong Clicks and number keys for the four lanes.
- **Terminal Simulator**: practice the terminals offline with a timer and best times (`/cc termsim`).
- **Simon Says Solver**: highlights the buttons in order, numbered above each one. It replaces Simon Says Lines.
- **Secret HUD**: how many secrets are done in the room you are in. **Left Click Secrets** is a module of its own now.
- **Score Calculator**: a score HUD for the run (clear, optionally boss), plus party messages at 270 / 300 and when the mimic, prince or bat is killed. The Paul perk (+10 score) is counted; **Force Paul Perk** (Dungeon Map) assumes it.
- **F7 Titles**: titles for terminals, devices, levers, the gate and the boss phases (Maxor Stunned, Storm Crushed, deaths, Goldor gate lines).
- **Class Colors**: a colour and small symbol per dungeon class, used by F7 Titles and other alerts.
- **Room Alerts**: a title when your room is cleared (with the time you spent in it) and when its secrets are all found.
- **Key Alerts**: a popup card when a Blood or Wither key is picked up ("Wither Key Picked Up").
- **Secret Clicked**: boxes the secrets you click, pick up or kill, for a moment. This is the part of the old Secrets module that drew boxes.
- **Dungeon Waypoints**: your own waypoints per dungeon room, saved relative to the room. Edit Mode: right-click a block to add one.
- **Tick Timers**: tick counters for the F7 boss (Maxor start, Goldor death ticks, Storm PY and pad), each its own HUD.
- **Mask Timers**: cooldowns for Bonzo's Mask, Spirit Mask and the Phoenix Pet, with a ready ping. An **Invincibility Timer** HUD counts down the invincibility after a proc ("Spirit: 3.0s", or just the time with **Invincibility Prefix** off).
- **Blessing Display**: the dungeon blessings you have, from the tab list ("Power: 14").
- **Warp Cooldown**: how long until you can start another dungeon run.
- **Run Profit**: tracks the loot and profit of every dungeon and Kuudra chest you open: HUD, history (`/cc loot`), Croesus chest profits.
- **Deployable Timers**: HUD with the time left on every flare, power orb and totem near you.
- **Ragnarock Timer**: the axe's 3 s cast, 10 s buff and cooldown, with a HUD and titles when it casts, fails or is ready. **Item Cooldowns** shows the same phases on the axe slot and has a ready / cancelled notifier.

### New: Slayers
- The Slayer Tracker now has a module per slayer: **Inferno Demonlord** (attunement, fire pits, fire pillar), **Voidgloom Seraph** (beacons, laser phase, Nukekubi skulls), **Riftstalker Bloodfiend** (Steak Stake, Twinclaws, Blood Ichor, Killer Springs, Château effigies), **Tarantula Broodfather**, **Revenant Horror** and **Sven Packmaster**. They need Slayer Tracker on.
- Your slayer boss is locked by its "Spawned by" stand, and the owner is shown.

### New: SkyBlock quality of life
- **Storage Overlay**: remembers your ender chest pages and backpacks and shows all of them in one searchable screen, from anywhere in the world. Pages sit side by side like the real menu, a page title opens the real page (`/ec N`, `/bp N`), and opening `/storage` shows it (Open With Storage Menu). Open it with a key or the Open Storage button.
- **Protect Item**: mark items so you cannot drop or sell them by accident.
- **Slot Binding**: link inventory slots to hotbar slots, then Shift + click to swap.
- **Wardrobe Keybinds** (armor sets 1-9) and **Loadout Keybinds**.
- **Waypoints**: mark places and see them in the world (box, fill, beam, name, distance, tracer). Chat waypoints vanish when you reach them.
- **Pet Display**, **Ping / FPS**, **SB Kick Timer**, **Skill Display**, **Armor HUD**, **Event Countdown**.
- **Stat Bars**: health, mana, vitality, overflow mana and defence from the action bar, each its own HUD.
- **Hub Map**: replaces the Hub world map with a custom image.
- **Item Info Tooltip** (item id, UUID, dungeon quality), **Item Rarity** (coloured background), **Enchant Colors**, **Tooltip Dates**.

### New: chat and screen
- **Chat Tabs** (tabs above the chat input) and **Smooth Chat** (new lines fade in).
- **Scoreboard Toggle** and **Tab List Toggle**.
- **Screenshot Preview** (a small preview with keys to open or delete it) and **Freeze Capture**.

### New: visuals and cosmetics
- **Custom Sky**: pin the sun and moon or follow your real clock, with an optional skybox picture cube from `config/cedclient/skyboxes`.
- **Title Background**: an animated CedClient background on the title screen.
- **Perspective** (look around your character in third person), **Sword Blocking** (the 1.8 pose), **Lava To Water**, **No Glow**, **Arrow Fix**.
- **China Hat** (Rainbow or Straw style, also shown on other CedClient players), **Custom Cape**, **ADHD**.

### New: utility
- **Command Keybinds**: bind keys to commands in categories (`/cc binds`). **Hotbar Keybinds**: extra keys for the hotbar slots.
- **Config Share**: copy your whole setup as a code, or import one. **Debug Panel**: an in-game log of what each feature detected and decided.
- **Mute Sounds** (silence sounds by ID, one-click mute for the last sound), **Cursor Tweaks**, **Mod Hider** (answers translation-key checks as if only vanilla and the server's pack were loaded).
- **Copy Item Data**, **Copy Entity Data** and **Dumpers** (save the scoreboard, tab list, action bar or chat to a file).

### Changed
- **Music** (was Spotify): the card has a seek bar and its buttons work in inventory screens. Party song lines that carry legacy colour codes are recoloured.
- **Dungeon Splits**: draws on the shared HUD card. New **Current Split Timer** HUD (Timer Format, Show Split Name, Suffix). Text Shadow, Corner Radius and Background Color are gone, Line Spacing is tighter and split names are plain by default. Times use equal-width digits, so they stop jittering.
- **Compact Tab** and **Compact Scoreboard**: the card look, with a CedClient footer in the accent. Custom name colours are shown as set, and with the Minecraft font Hypixel's symbols (ironman, bingo and others) no longer show as `?`. Panel Opacity defaults to 100.
- **Chat Alerts** HUD (Chat Ping) is movable and no longer fixed to the screen centre.
- **Leap Counter**: wider spacing between the count and the text. **Leap Menu** has a Menu Opacity setting.
- **Render Optimizer**: new No Falling Blocks, Clean Loading Screen, No Healer Fairy and No Soulweaver Skulls.
- **Chat Features**: Compact Range for repeated messages.
- **Time HUD**, **Chat Channel HUD**, **Daily Tasks** and **Critter Safari** are on the shared HUD look.
- **Keep Connection** is on by default.
- **Cosmetics**: the China Hat style is synced from the website too.

### Removed
- **Borderless Fullscreen**: Minecraft 26.3 has it built in.
- **Reset Panels** module: use Mod Settings > Reset Panels.
- **Secrets**: split into Secret Clicked, Left Click Secrets and Secret HUD. **Simon Says Lines** is now Simon Says Solver.
- The **Use NanoVG Rendering** toggles of Time HUD and Daily Tasks (they always use the new look).
- HUD Style, Menu Style, Rounded HUD Corners, HUD Outlines and per-HUD text outline: there is one look now. GUI Accent and HUD Accent became one Accent.
